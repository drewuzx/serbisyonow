# Direct provider downpayments

New bookings use the provider's saved receiving QR instead of a platform PayMongo checkout. The downpayment remains 30% of the confirmed total. The other 70% is due directly to the provider after service.

## Provider setup

Open Provider > Profile > Downpayment Account. Select the receiving app, enter the account name and account/mobile number, upload a reusable PNG/JPG/WebP receiving QR (up to 5 MB), confirm ownership, and save. The recipient shown in the payment app must match these details.

The QR is uploaded by the provider, not generated by PayMongo. It must not be a temporary, single-use merchant checkout QR. Customers may need to enter the exact downpayment amount in their wallet app.

## Booking flow

1. The provider confirms the service price.
2. The customer reviews the price and chooses Show Provider QR. This locks the price and receiving-account snapshot for that booking.
3. The customer pays the 30% directly, then submits the receipt image and transaction reference.
4. The booking remains Pending with payment Awaiting Verification.
5. The provider opens Review Payment and checks the actual wallet/bank transaction, amount and reference. Confirm Payment moves the booking to Upcoming. Reject Receipt requires a reason and allows the customer to resubmit proof without paying again.

A screenshot is not automatic proof of funds. There is no automatic wallet verification or money movement in this flow. Bank/wallet charges and limits, if any, are determined by the receiving/sending services; do not advertise the flow as universally free.

Receipts are stored in PostgreSQL and served only to the booking's authenticated customer or assigned provider. QR images are persisted in the existing upload storage, including its database fallback, so Render filesystem redeploys do not lose them.

## Existing bookings

Already-started PayMongo checkouts and paid bookings keep their original gateway flow. Untouched pending downpayment bookings switch to provider QR. Do not remove PayMongo keys/webhook while gateway payments still exist.

Provider profile QR edits do not redirect payments customers already started. New payment agreements use the current QR. Cancelled bookings cannot be verified; paid or submitted transfers require direct refund review between provider and customer. Refunds are not automatic.

The migration in `scripts/payment-schema.sql` runs on server startup. No account reset is needed.
