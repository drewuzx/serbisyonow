Terms of Services

These terms explain the basic rules for using SerbisyoNow as a customer, service provider, or administrator.

Use of the Platform

SerbisyoNow helps customers find local service providers in Angeles City and helps providers manage service requests, bookings, profiles, and verification details. Users must provide accurate information and use the system only for lawful service marketplace activities.

Accounts and Verification

  • Customers and providers may be required to submit identity information before protected features are enabled.
  • Providers may need to submit service details, experience, resume, documents, and complete skill assessment checks.
  • Admins may verify, decline, or delete declined submissions based on the information submitted.

Bookings and Services

Customers are responsible for reviewing provider details before booking. Providers are responsible for keeping service details, prices, and availability accurate. Final service scope, schedule, and payment arrangements should be clear before work begins.

User Conduct

  • Do not submit false documents, impersonate another person, or misuse another account.
  • Do not harass, scam, threaten, or discriminate against customers, providers, or admins.
  • Do not upload unsafe, illegal, or unrelated files.
  • Do not attempt to damage, bypass, or overload the system.

Reviews, Feedback, and Reports

Reviews and feedback should be honest and based on actual service experiences. Admins may review complaints, positive feedback, reports, and account activity to protect users and improve the marketplace.

Changes and Availability

SerbisyoNow may update features, categories, verification rules, and these terms as the system improves. Some features may be unavailable during maintenance, deployment, or technical issues.

Contact

For questions about these terms, contact the SerbisyoNow administrator through the official support channel listed in the system.
