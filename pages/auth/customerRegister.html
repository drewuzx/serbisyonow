Privacy Notice & ID Verification

Please read this notice before agreeing to submit your ID for customer verification.

1. Information collected

SerbisyoNow collects the ID type, address details, and the front and back images of the ID you submit, together with the account details you provide during registration.

2. Why it is used

Your ID and account details are used to verify your customer account and help protect the marketplace from false or impersonated accounts. They are not used as public profile content.

3. Who may review it

Authorized SerbisyoNow administrators may access the submitted documents for verification and administrative review. Other customers and providers cannot view your ID images on your public profile.

4. Your choice

Submitting this consent is required to complete customer registration. You may cancel now and return to the form without submitting. For privacy questions or data requests, use the support channel listed in the Privacy Policy.

5. Full Privacy Policy

Read the full Privacy Policy for more information about account data, uploaded documents, and location information.
